# Versioning styles

There are _many_ ways to version your package, {term}`SemVer`, {term}`CalVer`, {term}`EffVer`, {term}`ZeroVer`, {term}`Single Digit`, ...

You should choose the one that captures how you release your code. My rule of thumb:

> Is your package huge (e.g. Dask)? If _not_, then use {term}`SemVer`. Otherwise, use {term}`CalVer`.
>
> -- Angus' Rule of Thumb
