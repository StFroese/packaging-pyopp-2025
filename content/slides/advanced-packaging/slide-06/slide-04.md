# Alternative Python runtimes

There are other Python interpreters, like `pypy`.

There are also _web_ distributions of Python, like `pyodide`, `emscripten-forge`, `transcrypt`, etc.

We'll look at `pyodide` and `emscripten-forge`.

Emscripten-forge is very similar! Both have ways to pre-install packages.
